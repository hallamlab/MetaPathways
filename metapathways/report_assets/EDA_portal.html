CSV export includes every matching row and your selected columns, not just this page. Missing values stay blank. Formula-like text is prefixed with an apostrophe for spreadsheet safety. Counts in different tables describe different units; do not sum duplicated annotation or pathway rows as gene counts.

Pathway absence, unavailable output and expected MAG inference failures are different conditions. See Import notes and Execution history. MAG input genes are not a complete contig-to-MAG map. Source abundance values are not recomputed or certified by this report.
